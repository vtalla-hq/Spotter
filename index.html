    Each effect, cutoff and action links to one of these. Where Spotter says something without a citation, it's standard lab physiology or a common-sense step, not a research finding.

      How triage works. A marker is flagged when it's outside its typical adult range, or far from its guideline target for lipids and glucose. Spotter then checks your stack and your training for anything known to push that marker the same way. If something does and the effect is a lab artifact, it's filed as explained. If the effect is real harm, it's filed as a real risk. If nothing explains it, it's unexplained. A small set of thresholds are red flags whatever the cause.

      Ranges. Typical adult ranges used by Canadian labs. IGF-1, LH and estradiol depend heavily on age, sex and cycle phase, so your lab's printed range comes first.

      Spotter is an educational tool, not medical advice, and it is not a guide to using any drug. Many listed compounds are unapproved, illegal to sell for human use, or veterinary products with little human data. If you have chest pain, yellow eyes or skin, dark urine with muscle pain, or sudden swelling or shortness of breath, get urgent care.
